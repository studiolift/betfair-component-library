--- title: Head to Head date: 2024-01-09 tags: shortcodes ---

A shortcode to display a half donut chart to visualise the odds for home, away and draw.

Shortcode example

{% highlight html %} [head_to_head_chart id="1.265234886"] {% endhighlight %}
Parameter Usage Required
id The exchange market ID for the data. Yes
title Optional title to override the default one from the Exchange. No
subtitle Optional subtitle to provide additional context. No
home_color The hex color to use for the home team in the chart. Example: #BADA55 No
away_color The hex color to use for the away team in the chart. Example: #BADA55 No
button_text The text to display on the button linking to the exchange market. No
exc_domain The domain of the exchange market to allow you to specify international markets. No

Output example

Man City v Fulham

English Premier League, Saturday 21 Nov 2026 at 12:30

  • 70% Man City
  • 12% Fulham
  • 18% The Draw
% based on odds taken from Betfair Exchange.
