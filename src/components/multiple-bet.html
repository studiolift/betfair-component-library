--- title: Multiple Bet date: 2024-01-01 tags: shortcodes ---

A shortcode to display a multiple bet with several selections and a add to betslip button. You can use the SBK tool to search for markets and obtain the necessary IDs.

Shortcode example

{% highlight html %} [multipleBet title="Premier LeagueMultiple" selections="924.533727057|1096,924.533727064|48324"] {% endhighlight %}
Parameter Usage Required
title The title of the multiple bet Yes
selections The selections for the multiple bet. Should contain at least 2 selections. Format: marketId|selectionId,marketId|selectionId Yes

Output examples

Multiple bet title

  • Outright Winner: Arsenal 1/7
    English Premier League - Sat 21 Nov
  • To Be Relegated: Ipswich 17/10
    English Premier League - Sat 21 Nov
Add to Betslip at 3.37
